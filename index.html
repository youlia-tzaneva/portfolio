I'm a UX/UI, product, and web designer with 5+ years of experience designing complex digital products. I bring curiosity, systems thinking, and a user-first mindset to every challenge—balancing business goals, stakeholder needs, and real human experiences.

 

As AI reshapes the way we build products, I'm thoughtfully integrating it into my workflow to move faster, think deeper, and design more intentional experiences without losing the human perspective.

Systems Thinking

Designing beyond individual screens

I approach design as part of a bigger system—considering how interactions, business goals, content, and user needs connect to create experiences that feel intuitive, scalable, and cohesive.

User-First Approach

Empathy backed by critical thinking

Great experiences start with understanding people. I combine research, communication, and thoughtful decision-making to uncover what users actually need.

AI-Facilitated Workflow

I use AI to speed up exploration, challenge assumptions, and improve decision-making—freeing up more time for strategic thinking, creativity, and solving meaningful problems.
